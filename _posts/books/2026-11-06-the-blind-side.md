## Part-3 : How *The Blind Side* Blinds Us” by Jeffery L. Bineham

I found this article online which is a **critique of the 2009 film adaptation** of Michael Lewis’s *The Blind Side that approximately complete the blind side story. The film actually extracts the scence shorts to makes viewers feel inspired by Michael Oher’s success while hiding the larger racial and economic barriers that made his early life so difficult.*

The this article main focus was argument about the claims and its contradictions of **The Blind Spot** , the claim of film  is that it shows compassion, but it may also make structural inequality seem less important than individual effort and white generosity. It also promotes a **“postracial” American Dream - the idea that racism is mostly in the past and that anyone can succeed through hard work, talent, and good character. So, its a game of two players white and black, where film celebrates the Tuohys’ kindness while frames Michael as someone who needs white people to save him, to minimizing the role of poverty, racism, and unequal institutions because of constitution and rights of each individuals and also from the perspective of a human being like Tuohys.**
